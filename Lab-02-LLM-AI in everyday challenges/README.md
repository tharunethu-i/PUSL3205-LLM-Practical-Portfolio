# Lab 02 - AI in Everyday Challenges

**Group 5: The Busy Student**  
**Proposed product:** NextUp AI

## Problem Statement

> University students need to balance lectures, assignments, group projects, studying, extracurricular activities, personal time, and deadlines. With so many things happening at once, important tasks can easily be forgotten or left until the last minute. Design a product that helps students organize and manage their time more effectively.

## Our Solution

**NextUp AI** is an LLM-powered student planner that turns academic information into clear tasks and a balanced weekly timetable. It brings deadlines, study sessions, group responsibilities and personal time together in one place.
